# Almsby
*Every product has a story.*

## The problem

Two compliance clocks are converging on small manufacturers at once. By the end of 2027, US retail point-of-sale systems are expected to accept new 2D barcodes (GS1's "Sunrise 2027"), forcing every brand selling through retail to dual-mark their products or risk failing at checkout. At the same time, the EU's Digital Product Passport requirements are beginning to phase in — textiles among the earliest categories — creating a second, overlapping compliance burden. Existing GS1/DPP compliance tools are built for enterprise supply chain teams with dedicated compliance staff. Small manufacturers — clothing and textile makers with 1-20 employees — have neither the staff nor the patience for enterprise software, and are left to either ignore the deadlines or hire expensive consultants.

## The solution

Almsby turns GTIN assignment, 2D barcode generation, and product story-telling into one simple, guided workflow — no GS1 jargon required. A maker sets up a product, gets a compliant dual-marked barcode ready for print, and gets a free, brandable story page the same barcode resolves to — sharing origin, materials, and craftsmanship with the people who actually buy the product. One system, two audiences: the retailer's scanner reads compliance data, the consumer's phone reads the story.

## Why now

Sunrise 2027 and the EU textile passport requirements are landing in the same 2-3 year window, creating a rare moment where compliance urgency and brand storytelling point in the same direction. 2026 is the critical data-readiness year — brands that don't act now risk scrambling in 2027.

## Target customer

Independent clothing and textile makers (1-20 employees) selling wholesale through boutiques, wholesale marketplaces (Faire, JOOR), or direct-to-consumer, who are digitally comfortable but have never touched GS1 standards or barcode compliance.

## Business model

Tiered SaaS pricing by product count (e.g., up to 12 / 120 / 1,200 SKUs), covering GTIN management, barcode generation, and compliance tracking. Story pages are free on every tier, carrying a "Powered by Almsby" badge that functions as a built-in referral loop to other makers.

## The path forward

Launch with clothing/textile makers, where dual regulatory urgency is sharpest. Expand into adjacent categories (furniture, other DPP-affected goods) and up-market toward larger manufacturers as the same underlying data model — already DPP-shaped — supports increasingly complex compliance needs without requiring a rebuild.
