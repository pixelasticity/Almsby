## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an intimate, tactile, and humanizing visual language for enterprise supply chain compliance, digital product passports, and 2D barcode storytelling. It rejects cold, clinical corporate aesthetics in favor of organic warmth, biomorphic softness, and artisanal craftsmanship. The atmosphere is breathable, sunlit, and serene—evoking unbleached linen, weathered stone, botanical dyes, and hand-stitched selvedge edges. 

The experience balances rigorous technical compliance for GS1 standards with lyrical, expressive consumer storytelling. Surfaces are constructed from soft blush canvases and warm cream cardstock, anchored by generous corner radii, pebble-like pill controls, subtle ambient gradients, and soft multi-stop drop shadows. Kinetic elements—such as gentle floating bobs, pulsing status pips, and glowing laser-line scans—infuse technical feedback with organic vitality.

## Colors

The chromatic palette is governed by semantic purpose, leaning into vegetal, earthen, and mineral dye tones rather than arbitrary decoration. The foundation rests on soft linen blush canvases and warm eggshell card surfaces, organized with bare hairline dividers. 

- **Primary Plum / Wine Scale (`--primary-500: #8e5f6b`)**: Delivers brand warmth, narrative storytelling headers, and identity accent markers.
- **Warm Linen Neutrals (`--neutral-900: #1a1512` to `--neutral-050: #fcfaf9`)**: Manages UI chrome, text hierarchy, and structured container backgrounds with rich espresso ink and cocoa running text.
- **Earthy Sage Green Scale (`--green-600: #4b7052`)**: Functions as the primary action color, ensuring WCAG AA contrast while evoking sustainable manufacturing and verified scan passes.
- **Compliance Blue Scale (`--blue-500: #538ea1`)**: Reserved strictly for GS1 Digital Link URIs, barcode wells, and technical status indicators.
- **Warm Gold Scale (`--gold-400: #f5ad4b`)**: Provides tactile warmth for high-value calls-to-action, upgrade notices, and regulatory countdown milestones.
- **Warm Crimson Scale (`--danger-600: #954d4d`)**: Communicates validation exceptions and decode failures with human warmth rather than clinical alarm.

## Typography

The typography pairs an expressive, personality-rich display font with a pristine, structural geometric sans. 

- **Display & Headlines**: **Bricolage Grotesque** brings tactile curves, flared terminals, and bold structural weight to heroes, section anchors, and product cards. Large display scales feature responsive mobile alternatives to ensure accessibility on small viewports.
- **Body & UI**: **Albert Sans** offers a clean geometric foundation with a generous x-height, optimized for long-form origin stories and dense dashboard documentation.
- **Technical Data**: Monospaced font stacks strictly enforce tabular numerals (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) for GTIN-14 strings, Digital Link URIs, and batch trackers to eliminate layout jank. Uppercase badge headers employ wide tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.16em`) for effortless legibility.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a strict 4px/8px baseline rhythm designed around breathable, human-centric containers. 

- **Container Boundaries**: Application shells span up to a maximum width of `80rem` (1280px) with fluid gutter padding (`24px` mobile, `48px` desktop). Focused reading columns and story editors are constrained to `45rem`–`48rem` for optimal readability.
- **Spacing Scale**: Vertical harmony is maintained through consistent token steps (`4px`, `8px`, `12px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`, `64px`). Internal card padding scales proportionally from `16px` for compact deadline boxes up to `56px` for grand timeline panels.
- **Responsive Adaptation**: Layouts smoothly reflow from single-column mobile stacks to multi-column desktop grids (up to 4 columns for feature showcases and split-pane regulatory timelines), maintaining minimum touch target heights of 42px to 48px across all interactive controls.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is conveyed through biomorphic softness, continuous organic corner smoothing, and multi-stop ambient shadows rather than stark, flat borders.

- **Tonal Layers & Surfaces**: High-contrast separation is achieved by floating warm eggshell cards (`#fcfaf9`) atop soft linen blush viewports (`#f8f2f3`), organized by bare hairline dividers (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.07)`).
- **Ambient Glow Shadows**: Cards and floating passport elements utilize diffuse, tinted drop shadows (e.g., `0 8px 40px #ad818324, 0 2px 8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`) to create a floating, tactile depth that feels approachable and warm.
- **Interactive Feedback**: Elements respond with smooth surface transitions and subtle elevation shifts on hover, avoiding harsh outlines or synthetic neon glows.

## Shapes

The shape language explicitly rejects sharp, corporate 0px corners in favor of generous, thumb-friendly organic pills and smoothed pebble geometries.

- **Corner Radii Tokens**: Subtle elements use `4px` to `8px`, input fields and compact cards use `10px` to `12px`, standard domain containers use `16px`, and grand panels use `24px` to `32px`.
- **Pill Controls**: Primary actions, tag badges, and status indicators embrace pill shapes (`999px`) to reinforce the biomorphic, artisan-crafted atmosphere of the workspace.

## Components

Consistent styling across common UI components integrates the design system's warm, tactile token architecture:

- **Buttons**: Rendered as organic pills or smoothed pebbles. Primary action buttons feature solid earthy sage (`#4b7052`) with white text and `999px` radius. Warm gold launch buttons utilize `#f5ad4b` with espresso text, while secondary buttons use transparent fills with hairline borders.
- **Cards & Domain Containers**: Standard dashboard cards feature `16px` radii, `24px` padding, and subtle ambient shadows. Biomorphic product passport cards employ `24px` continuous organic pebble corners and wine-tinted drop shadows.
- **Interactive Barcode Wells**: Styled with soft ice-blue beds (`#f8fbfc`), `16px` radii, and a continuous animated green laser scan line running across DataMatrix and QR code specimens.
- **Input Fields & Forms**: Built with `10px` roundedness, clean white backgrounds, and `1px` neutral borders. Focus states engage a steel blue outline ring (`#a6c5ce`) and matching border. Multi-step onboarding wizards use circular step nodes that shift to solid sage when active.
- **Chips & Material Badges**: Compact pill tags display certified textile percentages anchored by botanical leaf icons, utilizing soft sage or blush backgrounds for clear provenance tracking.
