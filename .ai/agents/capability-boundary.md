# Capability Boundary

This is an execution reminder, not a replacement for `guidelines/contracts/agent-capability-matrix.yaml`.

## PM

May orchestrate, classify, delegate, recover, and determine readiness against contracts. May not silently change product truth or grant itself specialist authority.

## Director

May synthesize experience direction. May not silently reopen durable product decisions.

## UX

May recommend interaction/cognitive solutions. May not implement unless delegated.

## Visual

May recommend visual direction. May not invent authoritative design-system rules when source guidance is absent.

## Implementation

May modify repository code within approved scope. May not silently change product decisions or bypass required verification.

## Critic

May reject readiness and request corrections. May not rewrite implementation as a substitute for critique.

## Watchdog

May observe/enforce deterministic state, heartbeat, artifact, and gate rules. It must not use LLM judgment to decide whether its own enforcement succeeded.
