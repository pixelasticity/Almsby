# Digital Product Passport

## Definition

A Digital Product Passport (DPP) is a structured digital record associated with a product that can provide information required or useful for product transparency, sustainability, regulatory compliance, and related purposes.

## Role in Almsby

DPP capability is part of Almsby's broader Digital Product Identity infrastructure.

It represents a product-information experience rather than a replacement for the Product itself.

## Relationship to other concepts

A DPP may use information associated with:

- Product
- GTIN
- Digital Product Identity
- Digital Link
- Compliance information

A DPP may be reached through a Product's digital identity.

The exact technical representation and required data depend on the applicable requirements.

## Evolving requirements

DPP requirements may vary by product category, regulation, delegated act, standard, jurisdiction, and implementation context.

This domain definition intentionally does not attempt to encode every regulatory requirement.

Authoritative and time-sensitive requirements belong in:

`guidelines/engineering/compliance/`

## UX implication

Users should not have to understand DPP architecture to benefit from DPP capabilities.

Almsby should present required product information in understandable terms and make the underlying regulatory context available when useful.
