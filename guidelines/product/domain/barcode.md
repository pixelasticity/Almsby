# Barcode

## Definition

A Barcode is a machine-readable symbol that physically represents information associated with a Product.

For Almsby, the barcode is an important physical-to-digital bridge.

## Physical role

A barcode is not merely an image generated by software.

It is a physical carrier intended to be printed, displayed, scanned, and used in real-world conditions.

The generated symbol must therefore be correct and reliably scannable using real hardware.

## Relationship to identity

A barcode can carry information used to access a Product's digital identity.

For relevant Almsby workflows, this may include a GS1 Digital Link.

## Barcode is not

A barcode is not:

- The Product itself
- The GTIN itself
- The Digital Link itself
- The Story Page itself

These are related but distinct concepts.

## Quality implication

Visual plausibility is insufficient evidence of correctness.

Barcode generation should be validated through appropriate automated checks and, where required, real-world scanning.

## UX implication

Users should be able to produce a usable barcode without needing to understand barcode-generation internals.
