# Product

## Definition

A Product is a physical product represented and managed within Almsby.

It is the primary product-level object around which identifiers, digital experiences, Stories, and compliance information are organized.

## A Product may have

- A name
- Product attributes
- A GTIN
- A Barcode
- A Digital Product Identity
- A Story
- Compliance information
- A Digital Product Passport or related data

Not every Product necessarily has every capability.

## Product ownership

Product information should have a clear authoritative home.

When information belongs to the Product itself, other representations should reference or derive from that Product information rather than creating competing copies.

## UX implication

The Product should be the user's mental starting point.

A user should be able to think:

> “I'm working on my product.”

rather than:

> “I'm configuring an identifier record.”
