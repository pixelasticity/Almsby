# Story

## Definition

A Story is the meaningful information and narrative associated with a Product.

It helps communicate why a Product exists and can make otherwise invisible aspects of the product visible to customers.

## A Story may include

- Inspiration
- Craftsmanship
- Materials
- Process
- Decisions
- History
- Meaning
- Other details the maker considers important

The exact content is owned by the maker or business.

## Story versus marketing copy

A Story may support sales and customer connection, but its purpose is not simply to maximize conversion.

It should communicate meaningful information about the Product and why it exists.

## Authorship

The maker or business remains the author.

AI may help:

- Ask thoughtful questions
- Identify interesting details
- Organize information
- Suggest structure
- Improve clarity
- Offer interpretations for approval

AI should not silently replace the maker's voice with generic marketing language.

## Relationship to Product

The Story belongs to the Product's broader digital identity and public experience.

Product attributes that have an authoritative source elsewhere should not be independently duplicated in the Story merely because they are displayed there.

## UX implication

Creating a Story should feel like being helped to articulate something the maker knows, not like filling out a marketing questionnaire.
