# Business

## Definition

A Business is the organization, maker, or product owner using Almsby to manage one or more Products and their digital identities.

## Responsibilities

A Business may:

- Create and manage Products
- Associate identifiers with Products
- Create product Stories
- Maintain product information
- Manage compliance-related information
- Publish digital product experiences

## Relationship to Product

A Business owns or manages Products.

A Product should retain its identity independently of individual sessions or workflows used to create it.

## UX implication

Users should generally encounter their Products in the context of their Business rather than being forced to understand account, database, or organizational infrastructure.
