# D-001 — Compliance-first entry, broader product destination

## Context

Almsby operates in a space where GS1 identification, 2D barcodes, Digital Link, and emerging Digital Product Passport requirements create a concrete reason for customers to seek help.

Compliance therefore provides a practical entry point into the product.

However, making compliance the entire identity of Almsby would constrain the product to bureaucracy and infrastructure.

## Decision

Almsby should use **compliance as an important entry point**, while building toward a broader digital product identity and customer experience.

The immediate compliance problem should be solved exceptionally well.

The resulting product identity can then support useful experiences such as product stories and other customer-facing information.

## Alternatives considered

### Compliance as the entire product

Rejected because it risks creating an expert-only compliance dashboard and makes regulatory machinery the user's primary mental model.

### Storytelling as the primary entry point

Not selected as the initial product wedge because compliance and product identity provide a concrete, externally motivated job to solve.

### Generic QR-code generation

Rejected because it does not provide the standards-based product identity and compliance foundation Almsby is intended to provide.

## Why

The decision balances a concrete customer need with the broader Almsby ambition:

> Make product identity infrastructure complexity invisible.

It also supports the product promise:

> Every product has a story. Tell that story.

## Consequences

- Compliance capabilities must be reliable and trustworthy.
- The UX should not require users to become regulatory experts.
- Compliance should naturally connect to broader product value.
- Storytelling and customer-facing experiences should not feel like unrelated marketing add-ons.
- Product identity infrastructure needs to support more than a compliance checklist.

## Revisit when

Reconsider if evidence shows that the target customer, primary job, or commercial wedge has materially changed.
