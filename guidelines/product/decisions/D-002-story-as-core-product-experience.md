# D-002 — Story as a core product experience

## Context

Almsby connects physical products to digital experiences.

Compliance and product identity solve an important infrastructure problem, but the resulting digital identity can also create a direct relationship between a product and its customer.

For makers in particular, important product value can be invisible in the finished object: craftsmanship, materials, process, inspiration, history, and decisions.

## Decision

**Product Stories are a core Almsby experience, not merely an optional marketing feature.**

The Story should help a maker communicate why a product exists and make meaningful aspects of the product visible to customers.

## Alternatives considered

### Story as a marketing add-on

Rejected because it reduces the value of the digital product identity to promotion and misses an important customer-facing use of the infrastructure.

### Compliance-only experience

Rejected because it leaves substantial product value unused after the compliance problem is solved.

### Generic AI-generated marketing copy

Rejected because it conflicts with the maker persona and risks commoditizing the maker's voice.

## Why

The product promise explicitly connects product identity with story:

> Every product has a story. Tell that story.

A Story can also create a reason for customers to engage with a physical product after scanning it.

## Consequences

- Story creation deserves deliberate UX.
- The public Story Page is part of the product experience.
- The CMS should preserve structured content and authorship.
- Story experiences should support useful information rather than becoming advertising surfaces.
- AI assistance should help makers articulate their own knowledge and voice.

## Revisit when

Reconsider if user evidence demonstrates that Stories do not provide meaningful value to the intended audience or if the broader product strategy changes.
