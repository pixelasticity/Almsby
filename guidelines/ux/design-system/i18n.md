# Internationalization in UX

Internationalization affects interaction and layout, not just string translation.

## Principles

- Supported locales should preserve the same essential user task.
- Layout must tolerate translation expansion and contraction.
- Do not encode important meaning solely through English-specific word length or ordering.
- Compliance-related translations require appropriate human review where required.
- Locale changes must not silently alter product or compliance meaning.

The technical mechanism for localization belongs in `engineering/technical-architecture.md`; the user-experience rules belong here.
