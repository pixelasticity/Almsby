# Density

Density should follow the user's task.

### Storytelling
Allow room for product imagery, story content, meaningful typography, and breathing space.

### Creation/editing
Use focused density: enough context to orient the maker, controls close to the work they affect, and progressive disclosure for secondary configuration.

### Compliance/data-heavy work
Higher density may be appropriate, but grouping, hierarchy, and status must remain legible.

Ask: is this screen dense because the user's task requires it, or because the implementation accumulated information?
