# Imagery

For story-oriented experiences, imagery should help communicate the physical product, material and craft, context, maker character, and evidence of why the product exists.

Prefer meaningful imagery over decorative stock imagery. Give important images sufficient space, preserve aspect ratio, verify cropping responsively, and provide meaningful alternative text when imagery conveys information.

Do not invent a photographic art direction solely because generated imagery looks attractive. Tie imagery choices to the product/story job.
