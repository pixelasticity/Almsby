# AI-Assisted Authoring

## Problem

Makers often know their product deeply but may not know how to structure that knowledge into compelling, clear story content.

## Intent

Use AI to reduce the work of organizing and expressing product knowledge without taking authorship away from the maker.

## Pattern

1. Establish what the maker is trying to communicate.
2. Gather or discover relevant product knowledge.
3. Offer structure, questions, interpretations, or a draft.
4. Make the AI's contribution visible where interpretation matters.
5. Allow the maker to edit and approve.
6. Preserve the maker's voice and ownership.
7. Never present generated claims as verified facts without appropriate evidence.

## Guardrails

The AI should:

- amplify the maker's voice;
- avoid generic marketing language;
- avoid inventing product facts;
- distinguish suggestion from user-authored content;
- make meaningful uncertainty visible;
- keep consequential publication decisions with the user.
