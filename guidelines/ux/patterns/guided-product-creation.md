# Guided Product Creation

## Problem

A first-time Almsby user may understand their physical product without understanding GS1 terminology, identifiers, barcode standards, or compliance workflows.

## Intent

Help the user move from:

> I have a product.

to:

> My product has the identity and digital experience it needs.

without requiring the user to become a GS1 expert first.

## Pattern

1. Start from the product and user's goal.
2. Ask only for information that cannot reasonably be discovered or inferred.
3. Explain technical/compliance concepts at the moment they become relevant.
4. Handle repetitive or technical work automatically where safe.
5. Surface consequential choices with an explanation.
6. Show progress and the next meaningful action.
7. Provide recovery when an external dependency or validation step fails.

## Guardrails

- Do not begin with an intimidating compliance questionnaire.
- Do not expose internal implementation terminology as the primary mental model.
- Do not hide consequential decisions behind automation.
- Do not claim completion without the applicable verification evidence.

## Related principles

- Make the next step obvious.
- Hide bureaucracy, not meaning.
- Automate work; expose decisions.
