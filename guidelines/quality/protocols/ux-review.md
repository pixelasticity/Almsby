# UX & Product Review

For applicable changes, ask:

1. Is the user's intended outcome clear?
2. Is the next step obvious?
3. Has unnecessary complexity been moved out of the user's head?
4. Is the experience asking for information the system could discover or infer?
5. Are consequential decisions explained before approval?
6. Does automation reduce work without hiding important decisions?
7. Does the product preserve user understanding and control?
8. Does the experience preserve authorship and voice where maker-created content is involved?
9. Are compliance concepts introduced helpfully rather than overwhelming the user?
10. Does the result make the user's life easier?

Record relevant Constitution/product-decision checks and unresolved tensions between competing goals.

A UX review may identify a problem even when automated tests pass.
