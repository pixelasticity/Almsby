# Visual Review

A visual review asks whether a UI change forms a coherent Almsby experience rather than merely functioning technically.

Review:

- hierarchy and emphasis;
- typography and readability;
- spacing and rhythm;
- density;
- composition;
- color and contrast;
- imagery/media treatment;
- interaction states;
- responsive composition;
- consistency with existing patterns;
- fit with product and persona.

“Modern”, “clean”, “beautiful”, or “looks good” are not sufficient findings.

The review should state what the design is trying to communicate, whether hierarchy makes the next action obvious, where attention is being spent, what feels generic or accidental, and whether the visual choices support the product's emotional goals.

Retain relevant screenshots and a concise review with strengths, defects, and accepted risks.
