# Internationalization Verification

For applicable changes:

1. Compare the affected surface across supported locales.
2. Verify translated strings exist and render correctly.
3. Check layout expansion/contraction caused by translation.
4. Confirm locale-specific content does not silently fall back where that would mislead users.
5. Confirm human review of compliance-related translations where required.
6. Verify language changes do not alter underlying product/compliance meaning.

**Pass:** supported locales provide the same essential task without misleading omissions, broken layout, or materially different compliance meaning.

**Evidence:** retain locale coverage/check results and translation-review dependencies.
